# Contributor code of conduct

We aim to make participation in StageLedger constructive and welcoming regardless of experience or background.

- Be respectful, patient and considerate in technical discussions.
- Offer actionable, specific feedback and assume good intent.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing or spam.
- Respect contributors' privacy and do not post confidential event or customer data.

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using a contact channel shown on their GitHub profile. This is a short project policy, not a claim of affiliation with any foundation.
